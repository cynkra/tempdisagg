# Trade and Sales of Chemical and Pharmaceutical Industry

This data set contains the monthly and quarterly imports and exports of
the chemical and pharmaceutical industry in Switzerland (in in millions
of Swiss Francs) as well as their quarterly and annual sales (Index).

## Format

Each time series is an object of class `"ts"`. The number of
observations depends on the frequency.

## Source

Import and Export Data are from the Swiss Federal Customs
Administration. Sales Data are from the Swiss Federal Statistical
Office.
