# Methods for Temporal Disaggregation and Interpolation of Time Series

Temporal disaggregation methods are used to disaggregate or interpolate
a low frequency time series to higher frequency series, where either the
sum, the average, the first or the last value of the resulting high
frequency series is consistent with the low frequency series. Temporal
disaggregation can be performed with or without one or more high
frequency indicator series.

A good way to start is the introductory vignette:

[`vignette("intro", "tempdisagg")`](http://cynkra.github.io/tempdisagg/articles/intro.md)

Our article on temporal disaggregation of time series
([doi:10.32614/RJ-2013-028](https://doi.org/10.32614/RJ-2013-028) ) in
the R-Journal describes the package and the theory of temporal
disaggregation in more detail.
